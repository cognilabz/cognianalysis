System Understanding

management

Explain what the service does before drilling into technical catalogues.

The system supports customer onboarding. A client submits onboarding data, the service verifies uniqueness, calls KYC, calculates risk, decides whether the customer can be activated or needs manual review, stores the result and publishes a started event.

The useful business view is not a list of classes. It is the onboarding decision path: accept request, check duplicates, verify identity, assess risk, decide status, persist, notify.

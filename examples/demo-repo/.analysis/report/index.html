Core Assessment

Executive Summary

The repository implements a compact customer onboarding service. It accepts onboarding requests, validates customer data, prevents duplicate e-mail addresses, calls an external KYC provider, calculates a risk score, activates low-risk customers or routes risky cases to manual review, persists the customer and publishes an onboarding event.

System Purpose

Start and track customer onboarding with KYC-based risk assessment and customer status management.

# Business Extraction Skill

Use this skill when the repository assessment needs a deeper business/domain view.

## Output focus

- Capabilities and use cases
- Actors and roles
- Domain terms and entities
- Business rules, validations, decisions, calculations, status transitions, authorization and error behavior
- Business logic examples
- Function and use-case examples
- Evidence for every claim

Do not infer business behavior without evidence. If behavior is unclear, add it to `open_questions`.
